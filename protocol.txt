Important note for the Hyperscanning Experiment!

When recording the data (hyperscanning), in the EEG room, 
make sure the trigger/button is pressed once in the beginning and at the end.
Make sure both signals are sampled at 500Hz!!

When verifying the signal, DIN1 should be marked at the start & end

Make sure when setting the annotation in the NetStation,
annotations come in the following order
rest, sing, stry

The annotation screen should appear as below
1__, rest
2__, sing
3__, stry

When detecting bad channels visually during the preprocessing step, 
the numbers indicate the condition of the experiment.

In addition, bad channels from singal A should also be dropped from signal B
Precisely, set of all bad channels from singal A and B should be dropped.
